# Flood reports

You can search for basic flood report details in the Risk Management app, but to see the complete report you'll be taken to the Public app.

!!! note
    Make sure you are logged in to Public before viewing a complete flood report.

## Search for flood reports

Click on the 'Search for records' button on the dashboard.

![Search for records button on the dashboard](images/search-for-records-button.png)

Follow the guidance on [saved searches](saved-search.md) and enter the details of your search.

## Viewing a basic flood report

On the search results page, click on 'View details' next to the report you want to see more about. You'll see some basic details about the flood report.

## Viewing a complete flood report

On the basic flood report screen, click on the 'View the complete report' button.

You will be taken to the Public app to view the complete flood report.

!!! note
    Make sure you are logged in to Public before viewing a complete flood report.
